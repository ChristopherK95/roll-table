# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- While a roll is open, the window shows who in your group hasn't chosen yet.
- Your own row is highlighted. Hover any row to see the player's full name, choice and roll.
- New setting **Auto-open**: open the window when a roll starts always, only in dungeons and raids, only for items you can roll on, or never.
- New setting **Wait until out of combat**: a roll that starts in combat opens the window once combat ends.
- Drag the bottom-right corner to resize the window. Each look remembers its own size.
- **Lock position** is now **Lock window**, and also stops resizing.
- Escape closes the window.
- Fixed: rolls that finished before you saw them (such as after a reload) no longer pop the window open.
